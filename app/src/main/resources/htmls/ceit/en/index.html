CEIT

Centro Educacional Ivo Tramontina

Educação, inovação e história conectadas ao futuro

CEIT is located in former industrial warehouses in the downtown area of Carlos Barbosa. These spaces, which are part of the local industrialization history, underwent an important revitalization process in 2018 and were adapted for new educational and technological uses.

Currently, the site has classrooms, a robotics laboratory, administrative areas, an auditorium, and spaces for events, including a cafeteria and restrooms.

The Tramontina CEIT preserves the memory of the space and transforms it into an innovative learning environment, combining past and future. In this way, it creates an ideal place for education and the development of new skills.
