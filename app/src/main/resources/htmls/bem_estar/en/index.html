Wellness Services

Activities focused on health

Pilates, Nutritionist, Psychologist, etc...

CEIT offers specialized services focused on comprehensive care, understanding that your health is unique. We work daily promoting not only the absence of disease, but a true state of balance, quality of life, and physical and emotional well-being. We want to be by your side every step of the way in your quest for a healthier life. Learn more about how each service works:

🧘‍♀️ Pilates: works on muscle strengthening, posture improvement, flexibility, and balance. The activities are guided and adapted to the needs of each person, contributing to pain prevention and increased energy in daily life. It is indicated for people with stiffness or constant muscle pain, as a relief treatment.

🥗 Nutritionist: conducts a complete individual assessment, analyzing each person's routine, eating habits, and goals. Based on this, they develop a personalized and balanced meal plan with practical guidance for daily life. The follow-up helps with nutritional re-education, improved energy levels, enhanced health, and the development of sustainable long-term habits.

💬 Psychologist: offers a safe and welcoming space for dialogue and qualified listening. The service assists in the development of self-knowledge, the organization of emotions, and coping with challenges such as anxiety, stress, insecurities, and personal conflicts. Psychological support contributes to strengthening self-esteem, improving relationships, and promoting greater emotional balance.
