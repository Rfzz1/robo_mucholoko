ATF

Tramontina Employees' Association

The Tramontina Employees' Association (ATF) was founded on December 29, 1987, with the aim of promoting quality of life, well-being, and development for its members and their dependents. ATF operates by offering benefits and support across various areas of life, contributing to the personal and professional growth of Tramontina employees.

Education

The association provides educational assistance for different levels of education, supporting school and academic tuition fees, encouraging development and investment in the future.

Health

ATF provides healthcare services through accredited doctors, clinics, hospitals, and laboratories, as well as assistance with medical appointments, exams, hospitalizations, therapies, and dental care.

Social benefits

It also supports members during important moments in their lives through social assistance, legal advice, benefits for newborns, children’s events, and special year-end activities.

Mission

Promote quality of life, care, opportunities, and well-being for members and their dependents through benefits and initiatives that make a difference in their daily lives.
