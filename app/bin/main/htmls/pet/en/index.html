PET

Parque Esportivo da Tramontina

The Tramontina Sports Park (PET) is a leisure and social space created to provide sports, relaxation, and integration for employees, families, and the community. The site features sports courts, an indoor gym, a bowling area, and outdoor barbecue grills, offering leisure options and activities throughout the year.

The Tramontina PET also has two covered kiosks available for employee rental. They are ideal for gatherings, birthdays, and meetings with family or friends, offering greater comfort and protection from the weather. In addition, they are integrated into the leisure area, allowing guests to enjoy the surrounding space during events .

How to make reservations at the PET

Reservations for PET spaces are made through the T.Você platform, in the “Sports Park (PET)” section, or via chat in the “Sports Park Administrator (PET)” profile. To make a reservation, click on “New Venue Reservation”, choose the space, the date, and the desired time. The selected time slot is temporarily reserved for up to 10 minutes to complete the request. Finally, enter the number of people, a contact phone number, and accept the terms of use to finalize the reservation.

Important

Reservations for the Trattoria and Kiosks I and II are subject to review and must be approved by the PET administrator before final confirmation.
